Villa & Real Estate Photography on Koh Phangan

Villa photography, drone, video and full property marketing for Koh Phangan real estate.

Start a ProjectSee Our Work

CreativeLAB photographs and markets villas, land and property developments on Koh Phangan — interiors, exteriors, drone, property video, virtual tours and full listing-ready marketing for owners, agents and developers.

Approach

Listing-ready visuals for a competitive market

Koh Phangan's villa and land market moves fast, and most buyers or renters make their first judgment from photos alone. Weak or inconsistent imagery costs viewings before a single message is sent.

We shoot and produce property content built for how it's actually used — listing platforms, social media, and direct outreach — so a single shoot covers photography, video and social content instead of separate vendors for each.

Photography for Villa Rentals

Villa rental listings live or die on their first three photos — most guests decide whether to open a listing at all based on the hero image alone. CreativeLAB shoots villa interiors and exteriors specifically for how rental platforms display them: wide, well-lit shots that read clearly as thumbnails, paired with detail shots of the features that actually drive bookings — pool, view, outdoor living space. The same shoot typically produces a shorter cut for social media and a property video for listings that support video, so one production session covers every channel a villa is marketed through.

Photography for Real Estate Sales

Sales listings carry different demands than rentals — buyers scrutinize construction quality, layout logic and land context more closely, and often make a purchase decision without visiting in person, especially international buyers. CreativeLAB's real estate photography for sales includes architecture-focused shots that show build quality and design intent, wider land and boundary context where relevant, and drone coverage that establishes a property's setting relative to the coastline, road access and neighbouring plots — details that matter far more to a buyer than to a short-stay renter.

Visual Content for Property Marketing

Photography alone rarely closes a sale or a booking — it needs to sit inside a marketing system that actually reaches buyers and renters. CreativeLAB's Real Estate Marketing service takes the same photography and video and builds it into listing copy, social content and advertising, so a property isn't just well-photographed but actively promoted across the channels where Koh Phangan buyers and renters are searching — direct portals, Instagram, and paid campaigns run by the Advertising team when a faster sale or rental is the priority.

How Professional Photography Increases Property Appeal

The gap between amateur and professional property photography shows up directly in enquiry rates — poor lighting, distorted wide-angle lenses and inconsistent color make even a well-built villa look cheaper than it is, while consistent, properly lit photography lets the property's actual quality carry the listing. This matters more on Koh Phangan than in markets with heavier foot traffic: with fewer walk-in viewings and more decisions made remotely from photos and video alone, the visual presentation effectively is the first viewing.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with real estate agencies as well as individual owners?+

Yes — we regularly shoot for agencies, developers and independent owners, adapting delivery to each platform's requirements.

Can you shoot a property that's still under construction?+

Yes, for progress documentation and marketing; final listing photography is best once the property is styled and complete.

Do you provide drone photography for every property?+

Where it adds real value and is legally permitted — not every property benefits from aerial shots, and we'll advise honestly.

How long does a typical villa shoot take?+

Most villas take half a day to a full day depending on size and whether video and drone are included.

Can you help with the property's marketing beyond photography?+

Yes, through our Real Estate Marketing service — listings, social content and advertising built around the same visuals.

Do you stage the property before shooting?+

We advise on styling and can coordinate light staging, but full interior styling is outside our scope unless agreed separately.
