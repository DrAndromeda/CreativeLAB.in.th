Frequently asked questions

How fast can Google Ads start bringing in leads?+

Campaigns can go live within a few days of approval; meaningful data usually builds over the first 2–4 weeks as we optimize.

Do I need a website to run Google Ads?+

Yes, at minimum a landing page. We can build one as part of the project if you don't have one that converts well.

What's the difference between Google Ads and Local SEO?+

Google Ads is paid, immediate visibility; Local SEO (Google Business Profile, Maps ranking) is organic and compounds over time. Most businesses benefit from both.

Can you target specific areas of Koh Phangan?+

Yes — campaigns can be geo-targeted down to specific radii around Thong Sala, Srithanu, Haad Rin or wherever your customers are.

Will I own the ad account?+

Yes — the account is created under your business's Google Ads login, so you retain full ownership and history.

How is performance reported?+

A recurring report covering spend, clicks, conversions and cost per lead, plus a short written summary of what changed and why.
