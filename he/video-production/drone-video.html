Drone Video Production on Koh Phangan

Aerial video captured safely and within local regulations, for brand and property content.

Frequently asked questions

Is drone video always combined with ground footage?+

Usually yes, though standalone aerial-only projects are also possible.

What happens if weather doesn't allow flying?+

We reschedule rather than fly in unsafe conditions.

Can you fly near the coast?+

Yes, subject to local regulations and any restricted areas.

Is this different from your Drone photography service?+

Yes — this is video-specific; Drone (in Villas & Real Estate) covers still photography.

Do you edit the footage, or deliver raw files?+

We deliver a finished, edited product; raw footage can be provided on request.

How far in advance do you need to schedule a drone shoot?+

A few days' notice is usually enough, more during peak season or for complex flight plans.
