Restaurant Marketing on Koh Phangan

Food photography, menu design, social media and advertising for restaurants that want fuller tables, not just nicer photos.

Start a ProjectSee Our Work

CreativeLAB helps restaurants and cafés on Koh Phangan get found and fill tables — food and menu photography, styling, menu design, social media, advertising and websites built around the full customer journey, from discovery to reservation.

Approach

Marketing built around how diners actually choose

On Koh Phangan, most diners decide where to eat from Google Maps photos, Instagram and a friend's recommendation, often within minutes of getting hungry. A restaurant's visual presence carries more weight than its menu wording.

We treat food photography, menu design, social content and advertising as one connected system rather than separate purchases — so a dish photographed for the menu also works on Instagram, Google Business Profile and in paid ads.

Google Maps & Local SEO for Restaurants

For a restaurant, showing up correctly on Google Maps often matters more than any single piece of content — most diners search "restaurant near me" or check a place on Maps before walking in, and an incomplete or inconsistent listing quietly loses bookings before a menu is ever seen. This work pairs directly with CreativeLAB's Business & Local Presence service: accurate categories, complete photos, and an actively managed profile that reflects current hours, menu and offers.

Restaurant Video & Reels

Short-form video consistently outperforms static photos for restaurant discovery — a well-shot Reel of a dish being plated or a room filling up in the evening does more to convert a hungry scroller than a menu photo alone. CreativeLAB produces short vertical video alongside food photography shoots, so a single production day covers both formats rather than requiring a separate video booking.

Content That Turns Views Into Customers

Good food photography and video only pay off if they lead somewhere — a Google Business Profile with current photos, a social account that's actively posting, or a booking link that's easy to find. CreativeLAB treats content, local SEO and social media as one connected system for restaurant clients specifically because the gap between "nice photos" and "fuller tables" is usually the distribution and follow-through, not the photography itself.
