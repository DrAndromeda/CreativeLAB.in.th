Frequently asked questions

Do you need an existing brand identity to start?+

It helps significantly; we can also develop one first through our Branding service if you don't have one.

Can you design social media templates we can reuse ourselves?+

Yes, editable templates can be part of the deliverable.

What file formats do we receive?+

Print-ready files for physical materials, and web-optimized files for digital use.

Do you handle the printing itself?+

We prepare print-ready files; printing is typically handled by a local printer of your choice.

How quickly can a single asset be turned around?+

Simple assets can often be delivered within a few days; larger campaign sets take longer.

Can this include packaging design?+

Yes, packaging design is part of our graphic design scope.
