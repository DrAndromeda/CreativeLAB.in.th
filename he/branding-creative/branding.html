Strategy, visual identity and applications built as one coherent brand system.

  • Visual identity

Frequently asked questions

How many logo concepts will we see?+

Typically 2–3 distinct directions before refining the chosen one.

Do you research our competitors first?+

Yes, competitive and market context inform the strategy phase.

What files do we receive at the end?+

Logo files in all standard formats, plus a guidelines document covering color, type and usage.

Can you also apply the brand to our existing website?+

Yes, through our Web Design or Redesign services.

How many revision rounds are included?+

Typically two structured rounds at the concept stage.

Do you trademark the logo for us?+

No — trademark registration should go through a qualified legal professional; we can advise on next steps.
