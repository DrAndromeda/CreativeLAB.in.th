Google Reviews Management on Koh Phangan

A steady request process and thoughtful responses — never fabricated reviews.

Request a QuoteSee Our Work

We help Koh Phangan businesses build a steady flow of genuine reviews and respond to them properly — a simple request process for happy customers, thoughtful responses (including to criticism), and ongoing monitoring across platforms.

What's included

  • Review request process

    A simple, ethical system for asking satisfied customers to leave a review.

  • Response management

    Timely, professional responses to both positive and negative reviews.

  • Monitoring

    Tracking new reviews across Google and other relevant platforms.

  • Reporting

    Regular summaries of review volume, sentiment and response status.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get us fake or paid reviews?+

No — we never fabricate or purchase reviews; this violates platform policy and our own Truth Rule. We only build a genuine request process.

How do you handle an unfair or false negative review?+

We draft a calm, professional response and can advise on flagging it to the platform if it violates guidelines — we don't fabricate a rebuttal.

Do I need to approve every response before it's posted?+

We recommend approval for negative or sensitive reviews; routine positive responses can be handled directly.

Which platforms do you monitor besides Google?+

We can include TripAdvisor, Facebook and relevant booking platforms depending on your business type.

How quickly do you respond to new reviews?+

Typically within 24–48 hours, prioritizing negative reviews for faster response.

Can this help improve an already-damaged reputation?+

It's part of the solution — see our Reputation service for a more complete approach.
