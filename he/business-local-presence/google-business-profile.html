Google Business Profile Optimization on Koh Phangan

A complete, actively-managed Google Business Profile instead of a dormant listing.

Request a QuoteSee Our Work

We set up and optimize Google Business Profiles for Koh Phangan businesses — accurate categories, complete information, regular photos and posts, and a proper review response process, so your profile actively helps visibility instead of sitting dormant.

What's included

  • Profile setup or claim

    Claiming and verifying your profile if not already done.

  • Category & attribute accuracy

    Correct categories and attributes that match what you offer.

  • Photos & posts

    Regular photo updates and posts about offers, events or news.

  • Review monitoring

    Tracking and flagging new reviews for response.

Frequently asked questions

What if I've lost access to my existing profile?+

We can help you recover or reclaim ownership through Google's verification process.

How often should the profile be updated?+

Regular photo and post updates — at least a few times a month — keep a profile active and relevant.

Can you add products or services to the profile?+

Yes, where the profile format supports it for your business category.

Do you handle verification if Google flags the listing?+

Yes, we can guide you through re-verification if needed.

Is this useful even if I already rank well?+

Yes — an active, well-maintained profile also improves conversion once people find you.

Does this include responding to reviews?+

Review monitoring is included; response management is part of our dedicated Reviews service.
