Restaurant Advertising on Koh Phangan

Meta and Google Ads campaigns built around your offers, events and menu.

We run advertising campaigns for restaurants on Koh Phangan — Meta and Google Ads built around specific offers, events or menu launches, targeted to the right local and traveller audiences, with tracking to see what actually drove visits.

What's included

  • Offer strategy

    Choosing what to promote and to whom.

  • Ad creative

    Food photography and video adapted into ad formats.

  • Targeting

    Location, interest and timing targeting suited to a restaurant's audience.

  • Tracking

    Measuring reach, engagement and, where possible, visits or bookings.

Frequently asked questions

Which platform works best for restaurant advertising?+

Usually Meta (Facebook/Instagram) for visual, local reach, sometimes paired with Google Ads for active searchers.

Can you target tourists currently on the island specifically?+

Yes, location-based targeting can focus on people currently near Koh Phangan.

Do you need new photography for every campaign?+

Not necessarily — existing food photography and video can often be adapted into new ad creative.

How much should I budget for ad spend?+

Most restaurants start testing with a modest monthly budget; we'll recommend a realistic figure based on your goal.

Can this promote a specific event or one-off night?+

Yes, short-term campaigns are common for one-off events.

How is success measured for a restaurant campaign?+

Reach, engagement and, where trackable, reservations, redemptions or foot traffic.
