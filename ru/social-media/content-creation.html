Social Media

Social Content Creation on Koh Phangan

Planned photo and video shoots that give you a month (or more) of platform-ready social content.

Request a QuoteSee Our Work

We produce the photo and video content behind a strong social presence — planned shoots, creative direction and edits delivered in the formats each platform actually needs, from square feed posts to vertical Reels.

What's included

  • Shoot planning

    A shot list built around your content calendar and goals.

  • Photography

    Product, lifestyle and behind-the-scenes photography.

  • Video production

    Short clips shot and edited specifically for social formats.

  • Creative direction

    Consistent visual style, color grading and framing across every asset.

Frequently asked questions

How much content comes out of one shoot day?+

Typically enough for 2–4 weeks of consistent posting, depending on your posting frequency.

Do you supply models or talent?+

We can arrange this on request; otherwise content features your team, space or product directly.

Can you match my existing visual style?+

Yes — we'll review any existing content and match tone, color grading and framing.

Is video included, or is this photo-only?+

Both are available; most packages combine photo and short-form video in one shoot.

How quickly do I receive the final files?+

Typical turnaround is 3–7 business days depending on volume.

Do you handle the scheduling and posting too?+

That's covered by Instagram Management or Monthly Packages — Content Creation is the production itself.
