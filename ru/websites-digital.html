CreativeLAB designs and builds websites for Koh Phangan businesses — from single landing pages to full business and e-commerce sites — combined with technical SEO and AI search visibility so the site is actually found, not just well-designed.

Approach

A website is infrastructure, not a brochure

A website that just describes your business isn't doing much work. The sites that perform are structured around what a visitor actually needs to do next — book, inquire, order — and are built so search engines and, increasingly, AI answer engines can understand and recommend them.

We combine editorial design with the technical fundamentals — clean structure, fast loading, semantic HTML, proper schema — so the site looks premium and is genuinely visible where your customers are searching.

Mobile-First Web Design

The overwhelming majority of Koh Phangan's website traffic — tourists searching last-minute, expats comparing options on the go — arrives on a phone, not a desktop. A site designed desktop-first and adapted down tends to load slowly and feel awkward on mobile exactly where it matters most. CreativeLAB designs mobile-first from the initial layout stage, then scales up to desktop, so the experience that most visitors actually get is the primary design target, not an afterthought.

Websites for Villas & Hospitality

A villa or hospitality website succeeds or fails on its gallery and its path to enquiry — visitors want to see the property clearly and then reach out with minimal friction. These builds are structured around large, fast-loading photography (coordinated with CreativeLAB's photography team where relevant), a clear amenities and location overview, and a booking or enquiry path that doesn't bury the call to action behind unnecessary steps.

Website Maintenance & Optimization

A website's job doesn't end at launch — broken links, outdated information, and slow-loading pages accumulate over time and quietly hurt both user experience and search rankings. CreativeLAB offers ongoing maintenance and optimization for clients who want their site actively kept current and technically healthy rather than left untouched after delivery.

Frequently asked questions

Do you build the website, or just design it?+

Both — design and development are handled by the same team for a coherent result.

What platform do you build on?+

We choose a modern, SEO-safe, static-first framework suited to the project rather than defaulting to one platform regardless of fit.

Can you redesign our existing website instead of starting from scratch?+

Yes, through our Website Redesign service, with care taken not to lose existing SEO value.

Do you handle hosting?+

We can set up and hand over hosting, or continue managing it — we'll agree what fits your situation.

Is SEO included, or a separate cost?+

On-page SEO fundamentals are included in every build; deeper technical SEO work is scoped separately for complex sites.

How long does a typical website project take?+

From 2 weeks for a landing page to 6–10 weeks for a larger multi-page or e-commerce site.
