Approach

A brand is a system, not a logo

Many island businesses have a logo but not a brand — inconsistent colors, fonts and tone across their website, social media and printed materials that quietly undermine trust.

We build brand identity as a full system: strategy, visual identity and clear applications across every channel, then apply it consistently through our own web, social and print work so it actually gets used, not just delivered as a PDF.

Branding for Hospitality & Tourism

Hospitality and tourism brands on Koh Phangan compete heavily on first impression — a villa, beach club or wellness centre with a generic or inconsistent visual identity blends into dozens of similar businesses in the same search results and Instagram feeds. Branding work for this segment focuses on creating a distinct visual language early, before photography, web and social production begin, so every subsequent piece of content reinforces the same identity rather than looking assembled from unrelated sources.

Branding for Local Businesses

Restaurants, service businesses and local retailers on Koh Phangan often operate with a logo but no real brand system — inconsistent colors and fonts across signage, menus, social media and their website. CreativeLAB builds practical, usable brand systems for this segment specifically: simple enough to apply consistently without a design team on staff, but distinct enough to stand out from template-based competitors.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only design logos, or full brand identities?+

Full brand identities — strategy, visual system and applications, not just a logo mark.

Can you apply our brand across our website and social media too?+

Yes — our other services (web, social, video) are built to carry the brand system through consistently.

How long does a brand identity project take?+

Typically 4–8 weeks depending on scope and how many touchpoints are included.

Do you provide brand guidelines?+

Yes, a practical guidelines document covering logo use, color, type and tone.

Can you refresh an existing brand rather than starting from scratch?+

Yes — we regularly refine and modernize existing identities rather than replacing them entirely.

Do you design print materials too?+

Yes, through our Graphic Design service — menus, signage, packaging and campaign materials.
