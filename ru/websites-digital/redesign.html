Website Redesign on Koh Phangan

A careful redesign that fixes what's underperforming without losing your existing SEO value.

Request a QuoteSee Our Work

We redesign existing websites for Koh Phangan businesses — auditing what's underperforming, restructuring content and design, and migrating carefully so the SEO value you've already built isn't lost in the process.

What's included

  • Audit

    Identify what's underperforming in design, content and technical structure.

  • Restructure

    A revised information architecture based on the audit's findings.

  • Redesign

    Updated visual design consistent with your current brand.

  • Safe migration

    Redirects and technical checks so SEO value carries over.

Frequently asked questions

Will we lose our current search rankings during a redesign?+

Not if migrated carefully — proper redirects and technical checks are core to how we handle this.

Do you keep any of the existing content?+

Yes, where it's performing well; the audit identifies what to keep, revise or replace.

How do you decide what needs to change?+

Based on an audit of analytics, user behavior and technical SEO — not just visual preference.

Can this include a full technical SEO overhaul?+

Yes, often bundled together when a redesign is being planned.

How long does a redesign project take?+

Typically 6–12 weeks depending on the site's size and complexity.

Do you provide before/after performance data?+

Yes, we track key metrics before and after launch to measure the impact.
