Approach

Advertising that understands the island market

Generic ad agencies run the same playbook everywhere. Koh Phangan has its own seasonality, booking patterns and customer mix — from Full Moon Party traffic to long-stay expats house-hunting for a villa.

We build campaigns around how people actually search for and book things on the island: mobile-first, often last-minute, often comparing a handful of options in one sitting. That shapes keyword strategy, ad creative and landing page structure alike.

Local Targeting on Koh Phangan

Koh Phangan's advertising audience isn't one homogeneous group — it splits into distinct segments that behave very differently online: short-stay tourists searching last-minute on mobile, long-stay expats and digital nomads comparing villas or services over days, and a returning base of regulars who already know the island but are choosing between options. Running the same ad set at all three wastes budget fast. CreativeLAB segments campaigns by audience type and by area of the island (Thong Sala, Srithanu, Haad Rin, the north coast), so targeting reflects where a customer actually is and what stage of the booking decision they're at, rather than a single broad campaign covering the whole island the same way.

Advertising for Villas & Real Estate

Villa and property advertising has a longer decision cycle than most local ads — a guest or buyer is usually comparing several listings over days, not deciding in one session. Campaigns for villas and real estate typically pair Google Ads (search intent from people already looking) with Meta retargeting (bringing back visitors who viewed but didn't book), and point to a landing page built around the property's photography and video rather than a generic contact form. This combination is what most consistently turns listing views into direct enquiries and bookings.

Advertising for Restaurants & Hospitality

Restaurants and hospitality brands on Koh Phangan compete on visibility at the exact moment someone is deciding where to eat or stay tonight — which makes local, mobile-first targeting more valuable than broad reach. Campaigns for this segment lean on Meta Ads with strong food or venue creative, Google local campaigns tied to Maps visibility, and messaging built around walk-in and same-day bookings rather than long consideration cycles typical of villas or real estate.

Campaign Strategy, Tracking & Analytics

An ad account without proper conversion tracking is effectively flying blind — you can see spend, but not what it actually produced. Every CreativeLAB campaign is set up with conversion tracking from day one (form fills, WhatsApp clicks, bookings, calls), so budget decisions are based on what's actually converting rather than surface metrics like clicks or reach. Reporting is built around the numbers that matter to a business owner — cost per lead, cost per booking, return on ad spend — not a raw dump of platform data.

Why Local Advertising Matters

An agency running campaigns from outside Thailand can manage the platforms, but it can't see what's actually happening on the ground — a festival changing traffic patterns, a competitor opening nearby, a seasonal shift in who's on the island. Being based on Koh Phangan means campaign adjustments happen in response to real, current conditions rather than a monthly report reviewed from a distance, and creative can be reshot or updated quickly when something isn't working.

Frequently asked questions

Do you manage the ad accounts, or just design the creative?+

Both. We set up or take over your Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts, write and design the creative, configure tracking, and manage the budget week to week.

What budget do I need to start?+

It depends on the platform and goal, but most Koh Phangan businesses see a workable test budget starting from a few hundred dollars a month. We'll recommend a realistic number once we know your goals.

Can you advertise in Thai, Russian and English at the same time?+

Yes — we build multilingual campaigns and route each audience to a landing page in their own language rather than a generic translated page.

Do you also build the landing page the ads point to?+

Yes, through our Websites & Digital team — message-matched landing pages convert noticeably better than sending traffic to a generic homepage.

How do you report results?+

You get regular reporting on spend, leads/bookings and cost per result, in plain language rather than a wall of platform metrics.

Can you take over an existing ad account that's underperforming?+

Yes — we regularly audit and rebuild campaigns that were set up but never properly optimized.
