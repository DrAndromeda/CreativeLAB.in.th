Approach

Social media that fits how people discover Koh Phangan

Most visitors and locals find villas, restaurants and experiences on Koh Phangan through Instagram and TikTok before they ever search Google. A weak or inconsistent account is a real cost, not a minor gap.

We treat social media as production plus strategy: a consistent visual identity, a content calendar tied to your business goals, and formats — Reels, carousels, Stories — chosen because they work for your audience, not because they're trending.

Photography & Video for Social Media

Social content lives or dies on production quality — a phone photo competes for attention against professionally shot content from every other account in someone's feed. CreativeLAB's content shoots are planned around what actually performs on Instagram and TikTok for island businesses: short-form video first, then supporting photography, Stories content and carousel material pulled from the same shoot so a month of posting comes from a single, efficient production day rather than constant ad-hoc shoots.

Social Media for Restaurants, Villas & Hospitality

Different business types need different social strategies. Restaurants get the most value from frequent, food-focused content — daily specials, behind-the-scenes, Reels that make a dish look worth the trip. Villas benefit from a slower, more aspirational cadence — property tours, amenities, lifestyle content aimed at longer decision cycles. Hospitality brands more broadly (beach clubs, wellness centres, tour operators) usually sit somewhere between the two, mixing regular updates with occasional bigger content pushes around events or seasons. CreativeLAB tailors posting frequency, tone and format to which category a business falls into rather than running one template account-wide.

Paid Social Advertising

Organic reach on Instagram and Facebook has narrowed for most business accounts, which means even a well-run page increasingly needs a small paid budget behind key posts to reach beyond existing followers. CreativeLAB can boost or run Meta Ads using the same content produced for organic social, keeping brand voice and visual style consistent between what's posted for free and what's promoted — this work is coordinated directly with the Advertising team rather than treated as a separate, disconnected campaign.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only manage the account, or do you also create the content?+

Both — we can shoot and produce content, or work with content you already have, depending on the package.

How many posts per month is typical?+

It depends on the package, but most active accounts post 3–5 times a week across feed and Stories, plus regular Reels.

Can you manage TikTok as well as Instagram?+

Yes — our content production and short-form video work translates directly to TikTok when it's part of your strategy.

Do you handle replying to comments and DMs?+

Community management can be included in monthly packages — we'll agree response expectations upfront.

Will you use influencers to promote my business?+

Only where it fits your strategy and budget — we run this as a distinct service (Influencer Marketing) rather than bundling it by default.

Can social media work alongside paid advertising?+

Yes — our Advertising team runs Meta Ads using the same content and brand voice for a consistent presence.

Building a Strong Local Brand

A social account that posts consistently but without a clear identity tends to blur into every other business account on the island. Before production starts, CreativeLAB defines a visual and tonal direction — color treatment, caption voice, recurring content formats — so an account becomes recognizable over time rather than just a feed of disconnected posts. This groundwork is what makes monthly content packages compound in value instead of resetting the account's identity every few weeks.
