Restaurant Website Design on Koh Phangan

A website built around your menu, location and booking journey, as part of your restaurant's marketing.

Request a QuoteSee Our Work

We build restaurant websites on Koh Phangan structured around what diners actually need — the menu, location and hours, and a clear path to book a table or order, with the on-page SEO to be found when people search for a place to eat.

What's included

  • Menu integration

    Your menu presented clearly, easy to browse on mobile.

  • Location & hours

    Clear, prominent details for finding and visiting you.

  • Booking or ordering path

    A direct link to your reservation or ordering system.

  • Local SEO basics

    On-page structure that supports being found in local search.

Frequently asked questions

Can you integrate our existing booking or delivery system?+

Yes, we link to or embed most common reservation and ordering platforms.

Do you write the website copy?+

Yes, following our copy rules — no invented claims, clear and useful content.

Will the site work well on phones?+

Yes, mobile performance is a priority given how most diners browse.

Can this include online ordering directly, not just a link?+

Full e-commerce ordering is scoped through our E-commerce service; most restaurant sites link to a dedicated ordering platform instead.

Do you handle hosting and maintenance after launch?+

We can set this up and hand over, or continue supporting it — we'll agree what fits your situation.

How long does a restaurant website project take?+

Typically 3–6 weeks depending on scope and content readiness.
