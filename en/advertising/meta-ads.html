We plan, design and run Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns for businesses on Koh Phangan — from awareness campaigns that build a following to retargeting campaigns that turn website visitors into bookings.

What's included

  • Campaign strategy

    Objective selection (awareness, traffic, leads, sales) matched to your business stage.

  • Ad creative

    Static, carousel and video ad formats designed specifically for Meta's placements.

  • Audience targeting

    Interest, location and lookalike targeting built around your actual customer profile.

Frequently asked questions

Do you write and design the ad creative, or do I need to supply it?+

We produce it — from photography and video through to copywriting — as part of the service.

Instagram or Facebook — which should I run ads on?+

Usually both, through one Meta campaign; we adjust the split based on where your audience actually is.

What's retargeting and do I need it?+

It's advertising to people who already visited your site or profile. It's usually the highest-return part of a Meta campaign once you have enough traffic.

How much creative do I need before launching?+

We typically start with 3–5 ad variants to test, then double down on what performs.

Can this work alongside my organic Instagram content?+

Yes — paid and organic reinforce each other, and our Social Media team can run both together.

How do you measure success?+

Against the objective we agreed — leads, bookings, traffic or engagement — reported on a regular schedule.
