CreativeLAB helps Koh Phangan businesses get found locally — Google Business Profile and Maps optimization, review and reputation management, and consistent business listings — the visibility work that sits behind every 'near me' search.

Approach

The visibility work most businesses skip

Many Koh Phangan businesses have a website and Instagram, but an incomplete or inactive Google Business Profile, inconsistent listings, or reviews that go unanswered — all of which quietly cost visibility in local search and Maps.

This work is unglamorous but foundational: it's often what determines whether a business shows up at all when someone searches 'near me' — before any content or advertising even gets a chance to work.

Local Citations & Business Listings

Inconsistent business information across different directories and platforms — a wrong phone number on one site, an old address on another — quietly undermines trust signals that Google uses to rank local businesses. CreativeLAB audits and corrects a business's information across the directories that matter for Koh Phangan (Google, Facebook, Apple Maps, relevant local directories), so search engines see one consistent, trustworthy set of facts rather than conflicting listings.

"Near Me" Searches

Most local search traffic on Koh Phangan happens in the moment — someone already on the island searching "near me" for a specific type of business, usually on their phone, usually ready to act quickly. Ranking well for these searches depends less on content and more on the technical fundamentals: accurate categories, complete profile information, consistent citations and a steady flow of reviews — exactly the areas this service focuses on.

Turning Local Searches Into Customers

Visibility in local search only matters if it converts — a complete Google Business Profile with accurate hours, real photos and active review responses converts noticeably better than a bare-bones listing, even at the same search ranking. CreativeLAB treats local presence work as conversion-focused, not just a visibility checkbox, which is why profile management, reviews and reputation are handled together rather than as separate, disconnected tasks.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as SEO for my website?+

It overlaps — Local SEO focuses specifically on local search and Maps visibility, while Technical SEO (in Websites & Digital) covers your website itself.

Can you fix a Google Business Profile that's already set up incorrectly?+

Yes — auditing and correcting an existing profile is one of the most common starting points.

Do you respond to reviews on our behalf?+

Yes, as part of Reviews and Reputation management, in your voice and with your approval on sensitive responses.

How long does it take to see results in local search?+

Some fixes (accurate categories, complete profile info) help quickly; ranking improvements typically build over 1–3 months.

Do you manage this across multiple business locations?+

Yes, we manage local presence across as many locations as you operate.

Can this work alongside our website and social media?+

Yes — local presence, website SEO and social media reinforce each other and we coordinate across all three.
