Frequently asked questions

Do you offer agency rates for regular work?+

Yes — we can agree a standing rate for agencies with recurring listing photography needs.

How quickly can you turn around a shoot before a listing deadline?+

Rush turnaround is possible with advance notice; standard delivery is 3–5 business days.

Do you photograph commercial and residential properties?+

Yes, both — the approach is adapted to the property type.

Can you shoot multiple units in one development on the same day?+

Yes, and this is typically the most cost-effective approach for developers.

Do you provide floor plans or only photography?+

Photography is our focus; we can coordinate with a separate floor plan provider if needed.

What licensing do we get for the images?+

Standard licensing covers listing and marketing use; ask about extended commercial licensing if needed.
